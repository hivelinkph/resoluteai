Technology · The Engineering Process

How a Resolute system gets built.

01

SCOPE

Understand the operation, challenges and objectives. We sit with your teams, map the workflows and define what success measurably means.

02

ARCHITECT

Design the right AI infrastructure and safeguards — models, data boundaries, security posture and escalation paths, approved before a line of code ships.

03

ENGINEER

Build and train the custom system on your knowledge, your scripts and your edge cases — evaluated against the metrics defined in scope.

04

INTEGRATE

Connect with existing tools, databases and workflows — CRM, telephony, ticketing and data warehouses — without disrupting live operations.

05

SCALE

Monitor performance and expand successful applications — more languages, more campaigns, more lines of business, on evidence rather than hope.
